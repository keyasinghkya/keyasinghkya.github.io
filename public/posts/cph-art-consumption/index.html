@Copenhagen Contemporary
#

Soft Robots: The Art of Digital Breathing
#

v interesting to see a shorter + visual version of the exhibition text. i have never seen this before and i think its a good thing, even tho i feel people are generally getting less and less interested in reading but this way any possibility of inaccess can probably be dealt with 🤔

i really liked this work featuring the balenciaga pope!! for a second i was afraid it was ai generated but it was all filmed (i think filmed in stuttgart)

so beautiful, so gay!! instantly following the artist behind this work cuz all my fixations were met in this piece = korean, manhwa, queer hot women on bikes, lots of great visuals (all my favourite ways of producing visuals are used in this)

noted this one mainly cuz what the fuuuck is this setupppppp!! i need to learn more about led screens. this is incredible. i didnt care too much about the content tbh.

i dont alwaaaays love ephemeral/experiential works like this but i always love bubbles. very very cool to watch this choreography of machines making bubbles and the bubbles lasting/moving around as they would. like this interesting phenomenon of exerting control via these machines but somewhat losing control on exactly how long the bubbles last + where they move around in space. at least i dont think they could control that

spa day for machines sounded very funny, looked very nice and tight



CC Create x Monster Chetwynd: A Feather in your Hat!
#

SOFUCKINGCUTE!



James Turrell: Aftershock
#

i saw a james turrell work finallyyy after seeing him mentioned in many contemporary art classes. and it was worth it for sure :333 we werent allowed to take photos inside which is such a good choice on his part. i like the quaker, inner light sentiment in the background of his practise. i almost tripped at one point cuz i couldnt tell where the wall started (but also im just clumsy 🤨)

@Den Frie
#

Maybe we could both belong
#

very soft exhibition, i loved

haven't formed my thoughts on this yet, took photos to think later 😌 just felt like would be relavant to what i'm reading/doing now. also look at the size of these texts, wow

finally got to see a felix work irl!!! always hoped to. i forgot to take a picture of the work lol but i did take the giant paper with me

update: took a photo after all the folds the paper had to experience on its way to prague with me

beautiful jarring electronic noise in this installation, it was very pleasant

jasleen kaur is another artist i have been following in the last couple of years but didnt get to see her work irl until today :')))))) made me think a lot about how all of these objects have such specific and special cultural meaning to people who belong to or know the culture they are from. i wonder how anyone outside of knowing reacts to work like this. also wondering because this is the kind of identity art i want to see. its so refined and special and profound. the more you know, the deeper and deeper it goes

i saw this work in the posters of this exhibition - so soft, so lovely

cant get over how young this person is, and how tender this installation is

i deeply enjoyed this experience of being inside of a tissue box – perfect installation. quadrophonic sound setup made the crispiest sound of the fucking tissue leaving the box

i didnt take a photo of this installation, but was so very moved by the text

bonus: i loooved the building so much
